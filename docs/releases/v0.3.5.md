# Weirling v0.3.5 (2026-10-26)

Rankings that follow every interest you confirmed, starter packs that no
longer add broad topics of their own, and an Emerging card about your feeds.

## Before you upgrade

No action needed beyond the usual [upgrade steps](../UPGRADING.md). The new
ranking applies from the next digest.

## Ranking

- An article was compared with only your three strongest interest clusters,
  and with one representative topic per cluster. With many equal interests
  (any onboarding with confirmed topics), most of them never counted: every
  article scored about the same, and a Python tutorial could lead a digest
  for a reader who asked for kernels and compilers. Each article is now
  compared with every interest topic, weighted by how strong its cluster is,
  and "Why this?" names the topic that matched.

## Interests

- Subscribing to a pack added an interest named after it ("Hardware &
  Electronics", "Programming Languages") even when you had described your
  interests, which pulled in everything the pack carries. A pack now adds a
  topic only for readers with fewer than three interests, and onboarding
  removes the ones added before your topics were saved.
- Topic names are matched without case, so "Systems Programming" and
  "Systems programming" are one interest; your own wording is kept.

## Digest

- "Emerging this week" counted every reader's feeds on the instance and,
  with no history yet, called every topic new. It now uses your feeds only,
  and stays hidden until they have two weeks of history.
